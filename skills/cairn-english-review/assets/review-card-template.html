7 句典型错误对照
I am responsible the project
I'm responsible for the project.
responsible 后必须加 for
I will to develop and maintain it
I develop and maintain it.
will 后接动词原形，不加 to
I can't understand your mean
Sorry, I can't understand what you mean.
mean 是动词，要说 what you mean
Most of time
Most of the time
缺定冠词 the
Almost yes
Almost always.
中文直翻，native 不这么说
We have a communicate problem
We have a communication problem.
修饰名词用名词形式
It's still have the problem
There are still problems.
be 动词和实义动词不能叠用
